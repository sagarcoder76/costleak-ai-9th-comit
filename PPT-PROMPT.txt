Create a professional 11-slide pitch presentation for a project called "CostLeak AI".

ABOUT THE PROJECT:
CostLeak AI is an AI-powered company cost-waste analyzer for small and medium businesses, startups and agencies in India. Users upload their company expense and subscription data in an Excel file, and the system automatically detects where money is being wasted: duplicate payments, unused software seats, overlapping tools, sudden vendor price increases, expensive manual processes and repeated overtime. Every finding shows the evidence rows, the exact calculation used, a confidence score, a priority level and an estimated saving per month and per year. All calculations use rule-based Python logic, not AI guessing, so the numbers are transparent and auditable.

TONE AND DESIGN:
- Professional modern SaaS / fintech look, clean and minimal
- Colour palette: deep blue #0F2A4A for titles and headers, teal #0E9F8E for savings, red #D64545 for waste figures, white and light grey backgrounds
- Rounded cards, soft shadows, generous spacing, bold readable sans-serif font
- Large bold numbers wherever a rupee figure appears
- Maximum 6 lines of text per slide, use icons, avoid paragraphs
- Include simple charts or data visuals on the dashboard and impact slides

SLIDES:

Slide 1 - Title
Title: CostLeak AI
Subtitle: Find hidden money leaks in your company before they become annual losses
Small caption: AI-powered company cost-waste analyzer
Placeholders for presenter name, team name and date

Slide 2 - The Problem
Heading: Nobody owns the leak
Points: Finance sees invoices, IT sees subscriptions, HR sees overtime, but nobody sees the overlap. An invoice paid twice. 18 licences nobody logs into. Two design tools billed to two different cards. A vendor who quietly raised prices 40%. Each leak is small, but together they are 5 to 15 percent of monthly operating spend.

Slide 3 - Why existing tools do not solve it
Heading: Trackers record. They do not analyse.
A three-column comparison: Expense trackers show what you spent with no recommendations. Enterprise procurement tools cost more than the waste they find and take months to implement. CostLeak AI shows what to stop spending and works in two minutes from an Excel file.

Slide 4 - Our Solution
Heading: Upload Excel, get a ranked list of savings findings
Show the four-step flow: Upload, Validate, Analyse, Act
Then four example finding cards with large rupee figures:
Duplicate payment to a telecom vendor - 19,800 rupees recoverable
34 unused seats on a workspace tool - 3,740 rupees per month
Three cloud storage tools doing one job - 12,200 rupees per month
Manual invoice data entry costs 1,35,000 rupees per month, automation saves 81,000 rupees per month

Slide 5 - Demo and results
Heading: Real results on a real dataset
Three large KPI numbers side by side: 9.0 lakh rupees monthly spend, 3.0 lakh rupees estimated monthly waste, 28 findings detected
Caption: 36 lakh rupees a year, found in under a minute
Leave space for two product screenshots: the dashboard and a finding card

Slide 6 - How the detection works
Heading: Eight rule-based detection engines, no guessing
List as eight short icon items: duplicate payments, overlapping software tools, unused software seats, vendor price increases above 20 percent, high-cost low-usage subscriptions, upcoming renewals on under-used tools, manual process cost, repeated departmental overtime
Footer line in bold: The AI writes the explanations. Python does the maths.

Slide 7 - Every number is auditable
Heading: We show the formula, not a black box
Display this worked example large and centred:
Unused seats = 20 paid minus 12 used = 8
Cost per seat = 10,000 rupees divided by 20 = 500 rupees
Monthly waste = 8 times 500 = 4,000 rupees, which is 48,000 rupees per year
Add two supporting points: every finding shows its evidence rows, confidence score and priority, and all figures are clearly labelled Estimated Savings

Slide 8 - Prioritisation
Heading: Fix the 50,000 rupee leak before the 500 rupee one
Show four priority levels as coloured bands: Critical above 50,000 rupees per month or duplicate payment risk, High 10,000 to 50,000, Medium 2,000 to 10,000, Low below 2,000
Add the scoring weights: rupee impact 55 percent, data confidence 20 percent, ease of action 15 percent, recurrence 10 percent
Add the workflow as a horizontal arrow: Identified, In Review, Approved, Actioned, Verified

Slide 9 - Technology stack
Heading: Simple, free and production ready
Show as labelled icon groups: Backend Python and FastAPI, Data processing Pandas and OpenPyXL, Database SQLite ready for Supabase or Postgres, Frontend HTML CSS JavaScript and Chart.js, Reporting ReportLab PDF export, Quality modular rule registry with unit tests and Docker deployment

Slide 10 - Impact and roadmap
Heading: 36 lakh rupees per year of estimated savings on the demo company
Roadmap items as a timeline: bank statement and Tally or Zoho import, multi-month savings tracking to verify savings actually happened, renewal calendar with email alerts, benchmarking against similar companies

Slide 11 - Closing
Heading: CostLeak AI - stop paying for what you do not use
Space for a live demo link, a QR code and a GitHub repository link
Presenter name and contact details
Small disclaimer text at the bottom: Savings are estimates based on uploaded data
